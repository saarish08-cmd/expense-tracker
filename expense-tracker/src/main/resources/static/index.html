How it works

Three steps, nothing else

01

Upload your statement

Export a CSV from your bank's online portal and drop it in. Ledger reads it, cleans up the messy merchant text, and gets to work. No manual entry required.

02

Auto-categorized

A rule-based engine sorts every transaction into a category (food, transport, subscriptions) so you see the real shape of your spending at a glance.

03

Private by design

No bank credentials, no third-party APIs, nothing leaves your machine. Your statement never touches a server you don't control.
